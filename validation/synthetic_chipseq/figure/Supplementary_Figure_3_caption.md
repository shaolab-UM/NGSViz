**Supplementary Figure 3. Numerical validation of coverage calculation using a synthetic alignment dataset.** **A**, Design of the synthetic alignment dataset. Eighteen single-end, unspliced 20-bp reads were aligned to a 500-bp artificial reference sequence, `chrSynthetic`. Forward- and reverse-strand alignments and four predefined overlap patterns were included. **B**, Comparison of analytically expected base-level coverage with coverage calculated by ngsViz and BEDTools `genomecov` across the complete 500-bp reference. All three profiles were identical. **C**, Enlarged view of the predefined overlap pattern in Cluster B. Four reads covered positions 201–220, and one additional read covered positions 206–225, producing expected coverage depths of 4 at positions 201–205, 5 at positions 206–220, and 1 at positions 221–225. **D**, Absolute per-position differences between the analytically expected coverage and the values calculated by ngsViz or BEDTools. All 500 positions matched exactly, with zero mismatches, a maximum absolute error of 0, and a mean absolute error of 0.
